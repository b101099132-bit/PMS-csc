04出院後再入院Readmission After Discharge

定義：出院日起 30／90 天內因任何原因於本院再住院（急診留觀不計）。分母排除住院死亡者與尚未出院者；導入後另排除出院未滿 30 天（2 位）或未滿 90 天（14 位）者。導入後再入院診斷：傷口清創 2、肋膜積水 2、意識改變 1、A 型流感 1。Definition: any-cause readmission to this hospital within 30/90 days of discharge (ED observation excluded). Denominators exclude in-hospital deaths and patients not yet discharged; after implementation, patients with less than 30 days (n=2) or 90 days (n=14) of follow-up are also excluded. Post-implementation readmission diagnoses: wound debridement 2, pleural effusion 2, altered consciousness 1, influenza A 1.

05病人滿意度Patient Satisfaction

出院後問卷（2026/05 起），整體滿意度 1–10 分；推薦率 = 回答「非常願意推薦」或「願意推薦」之比例。引言為病人／家屬原文，摘自「最滿意經驗」與「其他意見」。Post-discharge questionnaire (from May 2026); overall satisfaction on a 1–10 scale; recommendation rate = proportion answering “very willing” or “willing” to recommend. Quotes are verbatim patient/family responses (original language) to “most appreciated” and “other comments”.

06病人流向Patient Flow

途徑 → 照護流程 → 拔管時機 → ICU 停留 → 術後住院天數 → 結果；帶寬 = 人數。導入前資料來自灌流紀錄回溯，僅記錄住院死亡，其餘異常事件未登錄。Approach → care pathway → extubation → ICU stay → postoperative LOS → outcome; band width = patients. Pre-implementation data were abstracted from perfusion records; only in-hospital death was recorded, other adverse events were not captured.

07恢復動能曲線Recovery Momentum

把恢復從「終點」改看成「軌跡」：以轉入加護病房為時間零點，六個恢復里程碑各在第幾小時達成（中位數與四分位距）。導入後有時間登錄之 CABG 病人。Recovery viewed as a trajectory rather than an end-point: hours from ICU admission to each of six milestones (median and interquartile range), post-implementation CABG patients with time stamps.
零點 = 轉入加護病房；脫離呼吸器的中位數落在零點左側，表示病人抵達加護病房時已拔管。各里程碑 n 不同（僅計有日期與時間者；2025 年回溯案多數只有日期）；下床走路與胸管全拔之 n 較小，請併同人數解讀。導入前（2025/04/15 以前）資料僅有日期層級，無法繪製此曲線。詳見延伸閱讀「恢復動能曲線」。Zero = ICU admission; the median for ventilator discontinuation lies left of zero, i.e. patients arrived in the ICU already extubated. n differs by milestone (only cases with both date and time; most 2025 retrospective cases have dates only); n is small for first walk and chest-tube removal, so read alongside counts. Pre-implementation data are date-level only, so no curve can be drawn. See the extended reading “Recovery momentum”.

08團隊改善歷程Improvement Timeline

2025/04/15 團隊成立以來，於心臟手術照護流程推動之 26 項改善與 3 個里程碑，依首例執行日排序。適用全部心臟手術，非僅 CABG。26 improvements and 3 milestones implemented in the cardiac surgery care pathway since the team launch on 15 Apr 2025, ordered by first-case date. Team-level practices, applied to all cardiac surgery, not CABG only.
落地天數 = 團隊決議日至第一例執行日；由團隊自主決定之 23 項中位數 3 天、8 項於隔日執行；需經院內醫材審議與採購者另計（最長 207 天）。逐項問題、對策與佐證見延伸閱讀「團隊改善歷程」。Days to implementation = team decision date to first case; median 3 days for the 23 items decided within the team (8 implemented the next day); items requiring hospital procurement review are counted separately (longest 207 days). Problem, countermeasure and evidence for each item are in the extended reading “Improvement history”.

09資料與方法Data & Methods

資料來源：導入後病例來自本團隊「手術病人管理平台」逐案登錄（2025/04/16 起）；導入前病例由 2020–2025 年體外循環灌流紀錄與病歷回溯建檔。所有數字為彙總統計，不含任何可識別個案的資料。

Data sources: post-implementation cases were prospectively registered case-by-case in the team’s surgical patient management platform (from 16 Apr 2025); pre-implementation cases were abstracted retrospectively from 2020–2025 perfusion records and charts. All figures are aggregates; no patient-identifiable data are shown.

恢復動能曲線：各里程碑時間 = 該里程碑之日期時刻 − 轉入加護病房日期時刻（小時）；僅計兩者皆有時刻者。改善歷程：依團隊會議紀錄與第一例執行之病歷整理；落地天數以決議日至首例執行日計。

Recovery momentum: milestone time = milestone date-time − ICU admission date-time (hours); only cases with both time stamps. Improvement timeline: compiled from team meeting minutes and the first-case record; days to implementation = decision date to first case.

納入範圍：單純冠狀動脈繞道手術（含微創 MICS-CABG），手術日 2020/03/06–2026/09/23；排除 CABG 合併瓣膜手術，以及同一住院內之再次手術（僅計手術量）。

Inclusion: isolated CABG (including minimally invasive MICS-CABG) operated between 6 Mar 2020 and 23 Sep 2026; CABG combined with valve surgery and re-operations within the same admission (counted toward volume only) were excluded.

手術室內拔管：拔管時間早於轉入加護病房；導入前病例依灌流紀錄與病歷備註判定拔管時機。即刻拔管流程：由心胸麻醉科依團隊 protocol 執行之麻醉與術後照護。

In-OR extubation: extubation before ICU admission; pre-implementation extubation timing was classified from perfusion records and chart notes. Immediate-extubation pathway: anaesthesia and postoperative care delivered by the cardiothoracic anaesthesia division according to the team protocol.

切點：2025/04/15 為 protocol 導入日；導入前包含 2025/01–04/15 的 7 例。統計：連續變項以中位數（平均值）呈現；比率以分子／分母呈現，未做假設檢定。導入後人數少，死亡與再入院比率會隨個案變動，請同時參考人數。

Cut-off: protocol launch on 15 Apr 2025; the pre-implementation group includes 7 cases from Jan–15 Apr 2025. Statistics: continuous variables shown as median (mean); rates as numerator/denominator; no hypothesis testing. Post-implementation counts are small, so death and readmission rates are sensitive to single cases — please read them together with the counts.
